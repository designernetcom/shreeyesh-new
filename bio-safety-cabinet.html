02Product overview

Protection built
around every sample.

Bio Safety Cabinets are enclosed workspaces that use forced air circulation through HEPA filters. They help protect laboratory personnel and the environment from pathogenic microorganisms when aerosols may form during processing.

This compact and space-saving cabinet protects the sample, the operator and the environment. It can be placed on a working table without requiring a separate room, plumbing connection or exhaust ducting.

03Specifications

Designed for
cleaner workflows.

  • Fabricated steel body with smooth powder coating.
  • Airflow path components made from 304 grade stainless steel.
  • 6 mm transparent acrylic on all four sides for clear viewing.
  • Withdrawable stainless steel sample tray.
  • Drop-down front access door with two hand ports.
  • Unique 4-way air distribution system for filtered air coverage.
  • Exhaust path includes suction fan, prefilter, HEPA filter and UV light.
  • Electrostatic Precipitator (ESP) uses pulse electric field technology.
  • Second HEPA filter treats exhaust air before release.
  • Observation lamp provides illumination during procedures.
  • Time totalizer displays the device operating hours.
