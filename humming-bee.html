02Product overview

Designed around
controlled oscillation.

Humming BEE is an advanced Bubble CPAP system designed to deliver safe and effective respiratory support for newborns and infants.

The system combines CPAP with adjustable amplitude. The clinician can set the CPAP and adjust amplitude while frequency is automatically adjusted depending upon the set extra flow.

Source claim: The supplied product information describes Humming BEE as clinically proven through randomised control trials in nine hospitals across India.

01 / MODE

Normal B-CPAP

One of the three modes described for Humming BEE.

02 / MODE

HFNC

High-flow nasal cannula mode included in the supplied product content.

03 / MODE

Adjustable amplitude

B-CPAP with adjustable amplitude for clinician-directed setup.

04 / SYSTEM

Optional compressor

The supplied content states that it can be provided with or without a compressor.

03Science behind the device

From natural frequency
to stochastic resonance.

The supplied product content explains that the device increases amplitude while keeping blended gas flow constant and maintaining the CPAP level set by the clinician. The comparison graphic shows the change from conventional B-CPAP to the modified CPAP chamber and extra-flow response.

01 / PRINCIPLE

Natural frequency

Each oscillating system has its own unique frequency at which it oscillates.

02 / PRINCIPLE

Resonance

When an external periodic force matches the natural frequency, the amplitude increases.

03 / PRINCIPLE

Amplitude

Amplitude describes the movement or travel distance of the oscillating system.

04 / PRINCIPLE

Stochastic resonance

A weak signal can be boosted by adding noise to the signal.
