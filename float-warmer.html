02Product overview

Precision warmth
for neonatal care.

Our Advanced Servo Controlled Radiant Heat Warmer is a robust and reliable neonatal care solution built on over 35 years of expertise in thermal management technology.

Designed with precision and durability, it ensures optimal infant temperature regulation through Delta T technology, delivering consistent and safe warmth under clinical conditions.

Delta T control helps create a stable and supportive thermal environment for newborns, particularly those at high risk.
