02Product overview

A trusted foundation
for neonatal care.

The First Care Warmer is the foundational model in Shreeyash’s line of infant warmers. Launched in 2008, it has achieved remarkable success, with over 50,000 units sold worldwide.

Known for its simple yet elegant design, the First Care Warmer has become synonymous with reliability, durability and ease of use.

Its robust construction and user-friendly interface provide an effective and economical solution for neonatal care in critical care settings.

03Key features

Simple controls.
Thoughtful safety.

  • Auto calibration facility for fault checking.
  • Servo proportional control for safety and accuracy.
  • Air and skin temperature displays.
  • Manual mode for heater control.
  • Biocompatible skin sensor.
  • Air sensor for double safety.
  • Infrared ceramic heater.
  • Baby bed with marking and X-ray cassette.
  • Rotatable heater box.
  • Tray tilting mechanism.

04Technical details

Made for a
controlled environment.

A unique continuous tray tilt mechanism with locking facility ensures the baby bed is kept tilted to the desired degrees selected by the clinician.
