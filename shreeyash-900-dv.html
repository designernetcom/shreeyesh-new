02Product overview

Simple to understand.
Ready for critical care.

Shreeyash 900 Ventilator is a dedicated Adult & Pediatric Ventilator provided with different modes of ventilation to assist the patient as per the need.

Shreeyash 900 DV is a Volume & Pressure controlled ventilator with advanced modes of Invasive & Non-Invasive Ventilation to treat patients suffering from various lung diseases.

The PRVC, MMV, APRV, CPAP Plus PS mode and the HFNC mode put it in the category of the most updated ventilators.

In 2019, Shreeyash Electro Medicals expanded its expertise into adult ventilator manufacturing by introducing the Shreeyash 900 DV. During the COVID-19 pandemic, the company was approached to develop a Made-in-India solution for the country's healthcare system. Before release, the ventilator underwent a six-month testing phase involving 30 leading intensivists across renowned hospitals in India.

Following its performance in clinical settings, the Shreeyash 900 DV was recognized as the “Best Ventilator in India” and awarded a grant of ₹43.5 lakhs.
