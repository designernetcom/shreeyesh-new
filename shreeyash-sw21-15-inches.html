02Product overview

Designed for precision.
Built for critical care.

SW 21 is a dedicated Neonatal & Pediatric Ventilator with pressure and volume controlled operation. Its time cycled, continuous flow respirator is designed for neonatal and paediatric applications.

The system brings together controlled ventilation, high frequency / flow chopping, Auto mode and non-invasive High Flow Oxygen Therapy in one focused platform.
