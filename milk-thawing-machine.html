02Product overview

Efficient thawing
with controlled warmth.

The Fully Automatic Milk Thawing Machine is a highly efficient system designed to thaw frozen milk quickly and safely. It uses an intelligent microcontroller-based system for precise temperature control, warming milk from -18?C to 37?C in 30 minutes.

A forced hot water warming system circulates water uniformly to avoid hot or cold spots. The machine can thaw up to 1500 ml of milk in one cycle and includes automatic shut-off and water-level safety protection.

03Specifications

Designed for
consistent thawing.

  • Water pump and heater for uniform warm water circulation in the cabinet.
  • Forced Hot Water Warming system for efficient milk thawing.
  • Warms milk from -18?C to 37?C in 30 minutes, depending on room temperature.
  • Intelligent microcontroller maintains the temperature set by the clinician.
  • Special platinum PT-100 sensor with steel capsule for accurate bath water temperature control.
  • 3-digit LED display shows the set and actual water temperature inside the chamber.
  • Accommodates six 250 ml stainless steel milk containers, 1500 ml total per cycle.
  • Stainless steel cage holds the milk containers securely during the process.
  • Front door with transparent acrylic panel allows access and visibility.
  • Closed-loop system circulates hot water for uniform warming.
  • Illumination lamp for observation during the thawing process.
  • Trolley with four castors, including two locking devices for mobility and stability.

Technical details

Safety built into
every cycle.

  • Servo-controlled system with maximum temperature setting of 38.5?C.
  • LED display shows actual and set temperature for precise control.
  • Feather touch keys for easy temperature setting adjustments.
  • Platinum PT-100 probe with stainless steel encapsulation.
  • LED indications for cycle status, temperature and water-level warnings.
  • Water-level sensor prevents operation during low water levels.
  • Automatic safety shut-off if water temperature exceeds 38.5?C.
  • Electrical rating: 230 VAC ? 10%, 50 Hz, maximum 750 watts.
  • Best operating environment: 22?C to 28?C.
