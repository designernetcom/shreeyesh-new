02Product overview

Cleaner air
for critical spaces.

The Alpha Miniature Air Handling Unit is a compact, portable and easy-to-maintain germicidal product for air sterilization. It is designed to help protect premises against airborne infection caused by bacteria and spores.

UV tubes are arranged inside a mathematically designed reflective chamber. Controlled airspeed and lengthwise airflow close to the tubes provide maximum germicidal power against bacteria, germs, spores and moulds.

The Alpha unit was tested under field and laboratory conditions, with trials across a wide range of gram-positive and gram-negative bacteria and moulds. Performance trials were also carried out in hospitals around Pune.
