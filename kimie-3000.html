02Product overview

Precision at scale.
Control in every cycle.

Kimie is a fully automatic pasteuriser controlled by intelligent microcontrollers. Inbuilt heating and cooling systems work with digital displays for milk and water temperatures.

The compact, portable unit works without special plumbing connections and uses a 15-liter closed-loop process, allowing water to be reused for the next cycle.

01

Closed-loop efficiency

Uses 15 liters of water in a reusable closed-loop process.

02

Cycle automation

Heating, holding and rapid cooling are managed in one chamber.

03

Practical mobility

Four castors, including two locking devices, support movement and positioning.

03Special functions

One chamber.
Three controlled modes.

Designed around a simple, monitored pasteurisation cycle for human milk banking teams.

01

Heating mode

Heat milk to 62.5 C +/- 0.5 C using controlled bath temperature.

>
02

Hold mode

Maintain milk at 62.5 C for a 30-minute pasteurisation hold.

>
03

Cooling mode

Cool to 25 C in 5 minutes, 10 C in 10 minutes and 4 C within 30 minutes.

>
04

Automatic drain

Drain hot and cold water after each cycle with overflow support.

>
