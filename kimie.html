02Product overview

Small-volume processing.
Big clinical value.

The Kimie 500ml Mini Pasteuriser is a patented innovation for the precise needs of human milk banking. It processes volumes from 10ml to 500ml in a compact design requiring less than three feet of space.

A standard electrical connection keeps installation straightforward, while intelligent microcontrollers manage the heating, hold and cooling phases for repeatable operation.
